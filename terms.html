The helpful things to know before you join a class.

Class information

Batch timings, fees, age groups and availability can change. Message or call the studio for the latest options.

WhatsApp enquiries

Sending a WhatsApp message starts a conversation. Your place is set once the studio shares and confirms the class details with you.

Safety and participation

Choose a class that feels right for your health and comfort. If an injury may affect your movement, speak with the instructor before class.
