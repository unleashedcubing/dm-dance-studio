Before you begin

A few things you might be wondering.

New to dance? You’re in the right place. Here’s what most people ask before their first class.

I’m a complete beginner. Can I join?+

Absolutely! Tell us your age, goal and preferred time, and we’ll help you find a comfortable place to begin.

Do you have classes for kids and adults?+

Yes! We have classes for kids and adults. Send us the learner’s age and a preferred time, and we’ll share the best available batch.

How do I find the fees and timings?+

Send us a quick WhatsApp message. We’ll share the latest batch timings, fees and the best option for your goal.

Can you choreograph our sangeet?+

Yes! Share your event date, group size and song ideas, and we’ll take it from there.
